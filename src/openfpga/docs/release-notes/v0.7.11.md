# v0.7.11 — faster Photoshop Flowey and Snowdin snow room

Undertale v1.08 and experimental Deltarune Chapter 1 remain the supported games.

## What's new

- **Photoshop Flowey runs much faster** in Speed mode, with all of its artwork intact. It's close to full speed but not quite there yet. [PR #93](https://github.com/zenibako/butterscotch-pocket/pull/93)
- **Snowdin's snow-patch room no longer crawls.** This is the room with the "WARNING: DOG MARRIAGE" sign. Its snow also draws solid again instead of in white stripes. [PR #101](https://github.com/zenibako/butterscotch-pocket/pull/101)
- **Rooms with lots of tiles draw faster.** [PR #102](https://github.com/zenibako/butterscotch-pocket/pull/102)

## Installing

Import the core ZIP in Pocket Sync, or extract it to the root of your SD card, merging the `Cores`, `Assets` and `Platforms` folders. Your prepared game data and saves from earlier versions still work, and so do v0.7.10 Memories. [butterscotch-pocket.zip](https://github.com/zenibako/butterscotch-pocket/releases/download/v0.7.11/butterscotch-pocket.zip) · [Game-data guide](https://github.com/zenibako/butterscotch-pocket/releases/download/v0.7.11/GAME-DATA.md)

## Known issues

- Some heavy scenes still slow down, including other Snowdin rooms and the Asgore, Mettaton EX and Undyne battles. [#87](https://github.com/zenibako/butterscotch-pocket/issues/87) · [#81](https://github.com/zenibako/butterscotch-pocket/issues/81) · [#82](https://github.com/zenibako/butterscotch-pocket/issues/82) · [#83](https://github.com/zenibako/butterscotch-pocket/issues/83)
- Memories and sleep/wake are experimental. Deltarune battle Memories can come back with missing party or enemy sprites. [#61](https://github.com/zenibako/butterscotch-pocket/issues/61)
