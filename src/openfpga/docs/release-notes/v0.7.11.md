# v0.7.11 — faster Photoshop Flowey

This release speeds up Undertale's final Photoshop Flowey fight in Speed mode and brings in the latest upstream Butterscotch changes. Undertale v1.08 and experimental Deltarune Chapter 1 remain the supported games.

## Changes since v0.7.10

- Photoshop Flowey's Speed-mode drawing reuses cached sprites, textures and vine rasters, and its idle arm, face and scenery draws run natively. Pipes, vines, attack cues and eye borders keep their artwork, including through camera shake and the intro fade. In Pocket testing, the vine attack went from **16.7 to 26.1** game updates per second and the eye attack from **19.9 to 27.8**. A steady 30 is still the goal. [PR #93](https://github.com/zenibako/butterscotch-pocket/pull/93) · [Issue #94](https://github.com/zenibako/butterscotch-pocket/issues/94)
- The runner groups drawables by depth, reuses friction direction vectors, and skips collision events that are verified to do nothing. These are general changes, not specific to Flowey. [PR #93](https://github.com/zenibako/butterscotch-pocket/pull/93)
- Upstream Butterscotch changes through [`ec4fc0b0`](https://github.com/ButterscotchRunner/Butterscotch/commit/ec4fc0b0): CI fixes and an Illumos build fix, with no change to the Pocket programs. [PR #92](https://github.com/zenibako/butterscotch-pocket/pull/92)

## Installation and Memories

Import the core ZIP in Pocket Sync or extract it into the SD-card root, merging `Cores`, `Assets` and `Platforms`. Existing prepared game data and ordinary saves can be reused; prepared data does not need converting. [butterscotch-pocket.zip](https://github.com/zenibako/butterscotch-pocket/releases/download/v0.7.11/butterscotch-pocket.zip) · [Game-data guide](https://github.com/zenibako/butterscotch-pocket/releases/download/v0.7.11/GAME-DATA.md)

The runner's Memory format remains at revision 10, and v0.7.10 Memories load. Memories and sleep/wake remain experimental and scene-dependent. Deltarune battle Memories can restore with missing party or enemy sprites. [Issue #61](https://github.com/zenibako/butterscotch-pocket/issues/61)
